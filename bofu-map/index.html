The premise

Bottom-of-funnel is finite. You can count it.

Here's the thing about bottom-of-funnel: it's finite. There are only so many ways a buyer in your category searches when they're close to deciding. Alternatives, versus, pricing, best-for, switching, integrations. You can count them.

Most companies have never counted them. They're producing content against a list someone assembled from a keyword tool in an afternoon, with no idea how much of the board they're leaving to competitors.

The Map counts them.
