The part nobody's measuring

Your buyers ask ChatGPT before they ask you.

When someone types “best [your category] for [their situation]” into ChatGPT or Perplexity, they get three or four vendor names and a recommendation. No click. No session. Nothing in your analytics.

You either got named or you didn't, and almost nobody knows which.

We measure it. Ten buyer-intent questions, run five times each, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude — 200 checks a month. You get a citation rate per question per platform, tracked from the day we start.

Then we go change it. The pages get written so a model can lift a clean answer out of them. And because AI answers mostly synthesize third-party sources, we work on those too: the roundups already ranking for your terms, the review-site categories, the threads where your buyers actually ask.
