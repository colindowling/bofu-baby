## What the call hands you

The homepage offer is a free 15-minute call: five high-intent searches your buyers are making right now, where you're missing from them, and whether the AIs are naming you or your competitor. Yours to keep either way.

## How that differs from the Map

[The BoFu Map](/bofu-map/) is the complete bottom-of-funnel keyword set, usually 40 to 80 terms, scored and prioritized, in five business days, for $1,500. The comparison on that page is: five terms on the free call, all of them on the Map. The call's citation check is described there as a quick look. The Map's is ten questions across four platforms, per term.

The [90-day build](/pricing/) is priced separately: 15 money pages, 15 supporting posts, 90 days, $22,500, then $4,500 a month.
