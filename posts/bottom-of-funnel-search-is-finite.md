## The searches that mean someone is shopping

Top-of-funnel content gets you readers. Bottom-of-funnel content gets you the people typing "[competitor] alternative" and "[category] pricing" into Google with a budget and a deadline.

On this site those searches are grouped the same way the work is grouped: alternatives and vs pages, pricing and cost guides, best-of and roundup posts, migration and switching guides, and integration and use-case pages.

## The list is finite, so it can be counted

There are only so many ways a buyer in your category searches when they're close to deciding. Most companies have never counted them. They're producing content against a list someone assembled from a keyword tool in an afternoon, with no idea how much of the board they're leaving to competitors.
