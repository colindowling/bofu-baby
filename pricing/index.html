FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Why does the price go down?

Because the work does. Months one to three we ship ten assets a month plus all the architecture. From month four it's four a month plus optimization. A flat fee would mean charging build prices for maintenance work, which is how most retainers quietly become a tax.

How is this different from an SEO agency?

Two things. We only work bottom of funnel — no awareness content, no traffic goals, no blog calendar. And we measure and work on AI citations, which almost nobody does. Plenty of agencies have added “AEO” to their site this year. Ask them to show you the methodology.

What's AEO, actually?

Whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude name you when a buyer asks them who to use. It's a different job from ranking — you can be number two on Google and never get mentioned — and it's increasingly where the shortlist gets made.

How do you measure something an AI says?

Ten buyer-intent questions, agreed up front and never changed. Each one run five times per platform per month, across four platforms. 200 checks. We report citation rate, not position, because AI answers vary between runs and anyone quoting you an “AI ranking” is inventing a number.

Do I have to buy the Map first?

No. It's optional and it's credited in full if you continue. Most people do it because starting a build without the full term list means guessing at the order.

When will I see results?

Citations can move in three or four weeks. Rankings take 60 to 90 days — pages indexed in month one typically don't show until month three. We'll say this on day one so month two doesn't feel like nothing is happening.

What do you need from us?

CMS access or a publishing path, someone to approve pages, product and competitor context for the first two weeks, and Search Console access. That's it. You don't need to write anything.

Who writes the content?

We do, and we edit everything by hand. The research and drafting are assisted — that's how 30 assets in 90 days is possible at this price — but nothing ships without a human who understands your category rewriting it.

What if we already have comparison pages?

Good. We'll audit them in the Map, keep what's working, rewrite what isn't, and wire them into the link mesh. Existing pages with some age on them often move faster than new ones.

Can we add more pages?

Yes, $900 each beyond the 15. Though in most categories 15 covers the terms actually worth owning, and more pages past that point dilute rather than add.

What happens after the three-month minimum?

Defend & Expand is month to month at $4,500. Step down to Monitor at $1,500 for reporting only, or stop. The pages are yours either way.

Is the guarantee real?

Yes, and it's work-free rather than money-back: if we miss the target we keep going at no charge for up to 60 days. We set the target off the Map, so it's measured against terms we both agreed were winnable.
