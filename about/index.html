About

We write for the people ready to buy.

BoFu, Baby is a bottom-of-funnel content engine for B2B companies. We build the pages your buyers read right when their casual interest turns into true purchase intent, and we wire every one of them to a landing page that turns the reader into a qualified lead.

The notes are written by Colin Dowling. His podcast is Modern Business Life.

Why it exists

Traffic is easy to report. Customers are harder.

Most B2B content is built to be read by as many people as possible. That produces traffic, and traffic looks good on a dashboard. It rarely turns into pipeline, because most of those readers were never going to buy.

The person who types "[competitor] alternative" or "[category] pricing" into Google is different. They are not browsing. They have a problem, a budget and a deadline. They are comparing vendors right now, and whoever answers their question well gets the first call.

That is the reader we write for. Everything we make starts from that search and ends at a page built to convert it.

How we work

One engine, from search to lead.

We find the searches buyers make before they choose a vendor. We write the content that answers them, in the formats that fit: comparisons, pricing guides, best-of lists, switching guides, integration pages and case studies. Each piece routes to a landing page written for that exact reader, with a short form and a clear offer.

Then we show you the leads that came out of it, not the traffic that went past.
