AEO specialist vs SEO agency

An AEO-only specialist tracks whether answer engines mention you. A traditional SEO agency works on rankings and traffic, and treats citation as an extra if it is offered at all. Being named when a buyer asks who to shortlist takes the pages those buyers read while choosing, built to rank and to be cited, which is the work we do.

What is the difference between an AEO specialist and an SEO agency?

An AEO specialist sells visibility inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude: which prompts mention you, how often, and in what words. An SEO agency sells organic acquisition: rankings, pages, links, technical fixes, and the sessions that follow. They overlap on "publish something." They split on what that something is for.

Mention tracking without the decision pages measures a hole. A traffic program that never publishes them fills the wrong hole. The table is how we separate the three offers, including where we sit.

What does an AEO-only specialist leave out?

The page. A mention report will tell you a buyer asked for a shortlist and you were not on it. It will not write the comparison, the alternatives page, or the use-case page that should have been cited. If those pages do not exist, the report is a reminder of a vacancy. Recommendations to tighten a boilerplate, add schema, or pitch a roundup are not a site. The roundups answer engines cite summarize pages that already exist.

An AEO-only hire fits when the money pages are already live and the remaining job is to watch the models and earn the outside mentions. It is a poor hire when the site is still a homepage and a blog. How a citation is earned, source by source, is how to get your B2B company cited by ChatGPT. Keeping every description of you in agreement is covered in why ChatGPT can't describe your company.

What does a traditional SEO agency optimize for?

Traffic, and the rankings that produce it. The calendar that follows is familiar: head terms, definitions, a monthly post count, a report of sessions and average position. That can be the right work when a company needs demand it does not have. It is the wrong work when buyers are already comparing vendors.

The searches that precede a B2B purchase are a finite list: alternatives, versus, pricing, best-for, switching, integrations. Bottom-of-funnel search is a list you can finish. A program sorted by volume skips it, because the numbers look small next to a category term. When an agency adds answer-engine work, it is usually another column on the same report. The pages do not change. You can rank for a definition and never be named when a buyer asks a model who to call. Ranking and getting cited are two different jobs.

Which one should a B2B company hire?

Hire for the absence you actually have. If buyers already find you on the decision queries and the models still skip you, you have a citation problem. An AEO specialist matches it. If you have sessions and no pipeline, you have a page problem: the content that earned the sessions is not what a buyer reads before a call, and continuing that calendar extends it. If you have neither the pages nor the citations, either specialist alone leaves half the job open. One will track mentions you do not have. The other will publish pages for queries that do not convert. The combination is the money pages and the citation on those pages. That is us.

"Full service" usually means technical cleanup, digital PR, and sometimes paid search sit on the same contract. The breadth is real. The center of gravity is still traffic. Extra services around a traffic goal do not make citation the product. If you already have that agency, ask which buying queries have no page from you, and which of those queries name someone else inside ChatGPT. A calendar of definitions is the answer. You do not need a louder version of it.

Questions about hiring either.

Should I hire an AEO specialist or an SEO agency?

Hire an AEO specialist if the decision pages already exist and the models still skip you. Hire an SEO agency if you want organic traffic across the whole site and you are willing to be measured on sessions. If you need to be present when a buyer is choosing, you need the decision pages and the citation work together.

Is AEO just SEO with a new name?

No. SEO is built to rank a page and earn the click. AEO is built so an answer engine names you when a buyer asks who to use, often with no click. The pages, the sources and the metric are different. A program can do both. Most SEO retainers do the first job only.

Can an SEO agency add AEO later?

It can add a report. Doing the work means changing which pages get published and which outside sources get pursued. A mention chart on top of a top-of-funnel blog is an add-on. It is not the same offer.

What is an AEO-only agency missing?

The money pages. Tracking prompts shows the gap. Comparisons, alternatives, pricing-intent pages and use-case pages fill it. Without those pages, and without third-party sources that repeat the same facts, there is little for the model to cite.

Do you replace our SEO agency?

Not by default. We do not run a full-site traffic program, technical retainers or paid search. Some companies keep an SEO agency for that and use us for the buying queries and the citations. If the current output is only top-of-funnel posts, replacing that output is the point.
