Ranking and getting cited are two different jobs

BoFu, Baby measures AI citation separately from rank: ten buyer-intent questions, five runs each, across four platforms. That is 200 checks a month.

31 Oct 2026By Colin DowlingPart of how AI citation is measured

Key takeaways

  • A buyer can get a vendor shortlist from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews or Claude without a click showing up in analytics.
  • Citation is measured as a rate, from ten buyer-intent questions run five times on each of four platforms.
  • That is 200 checks a month. The questions are agreed up front and do not change.
  • The pages are written so a model can lift an answer, and the work also covers the third-party sources those answers are built from.

Named, or not, with no session to show for it

When someone types "best [your category] for [their situation]" into ChatGPT or Perplexity, they get three or four vendor names and a recommendation. No click. No session. Nothing in analytics.

You either got named or you didn't. Ranking and getting cited are two different jobs.

What the 200 checks are

Ten buyer-intent questions, run five times each, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude. That is 200 checks a month. The report is a citation rate per question per platform, tracked from the day the work starts.

The questions are agreed up front and do not change. The number reported is a citation rate, not a position. Answers vary between runs, so an "AI ranking" is not a measurement this site uses.

What gets changed after the count

The pages get written so a model can lift a clean answer out of them. AI answers mostly synthesize third-party sources, so the work also covers the roundups already ranking for your terms, the review-site categories, and the threads where buyers ask.

Google position and AI citation rate are reported separately. They move independently.

Questions

What is AEO here?

Whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude name you when a buyer asks them who to use. It's a different job from ranking — you can be number two on Google and never get mentioned — and it's increasingly where the shortlist gets made.

How do you measure something an AI says?

Ten buyer-intent questions, agreed up front and never changed. Each one run five times per platform per month, across four platforms. 200 checks. We report citation rate, not position, because AI answers vary between runs and anyone quoting you an “AI ranking” is inventing a number.

Which platforms are in the 200 checks?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude.
